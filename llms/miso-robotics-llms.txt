# Miso Robotics

> Miso Robotics is a Pasadena, California kitchen-automation company building AI-powered
> robotics and restaurant operations software for commercial food service. Its flagship
> product Flippy is an autonomous fry station using computer vision and NVIDIA-accelerated
> motion planning; Zippy (built on the acquired Zignyl platform) is a real-time digital
> shift manager for forecasting, scheduling, task management, incentives and payroll that
> connects to restaurant point-of-sale systems.

## API surface

Miso Robotics publishes **no public developer API**. As of 2026-08-01 there is no
OpenAPI/Swagger contract, no GraphQL endpoint, no AsyncAPI or webhook catalog, no MCP
server, no A2A agent card, no SDKs on npm/PyPI/Maven/NuGet/RubyGems/crates.io, and no
developer portal, documentation site or API reference. Point-of-sale integrations
(Toast, PAR and others, inherited with the Zignyl acquisition) are private partner
connections arranged through sales, not a self-serve public API.

## Products

- [Flippy Fry Station](https://misorobotics.com/flippy/): autonomous robotic fry station; computer-vision food identification and doneness detection; up to 100 baskets/hour; works with Frymaster, Henny Penny and Pitco commercial fryers; POS connection, real-time monitoring and remote diagnostics.
- [Zippy](https://www.misorobotics.com/zippy): real-time digital shift manager — demand forecasting, scheduling, task management, performance incentives, reporting and payroll. Web app at https://app.zignyl.com/ (authenticated).
